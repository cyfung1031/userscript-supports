# Robustness and Anti-Rationalization Rules

## 1. Fail closed on meta-novelty

"This feels new" is not derivation authority. Require `NO_OWNER` or a concrete owner escape/gap before
`PICK_DERIVE`; explicit `PICK_AUDIT` remains non-compiling.

## 2. Fail closed on authority

PickInvariant cannot demote explicit authority or mature owner semantics merely to obtain a cleaner
representation, nicer quotient, simpler probability law, or more elegant theorem mapping.

## 3. Fail local on uncertainty

If uncertainty concerns one predicate, verifier, seam, stochastic law, composition rule, or topology
claim, contain it there. Do not reopen the whole procedure.

## 4. Avoid false Pick mappings

Do not force every domain into a fake area equation. In structural mode:

- `I` is not a count unless the domain makes it one;
- `B` is not weighted by `1/2` unless proved;
- `χ` is not automatically numeric;
- the target need not be additive;
- geometric terminology does not substitute for reconstruction.

## 5. Avoid false quotienting

Do not merge states merely because they look similar, share coordinates, or produce the same current
snapshot. Safe merging is target/domain/discriminator-relative and may need future-continuation or
stochastic-channel checks.

Conversely, do not preserve every raw difference. A distinction that cannot alter the certified
target should be removed after adequate merge/removal testing.

## 6. Avoid false topology

Use `χ` only when local facts plus direct seams are insufficient. Complexity, importance, large scope,
or "global" wording alone do not make something topological.

## 7. Avoid false composition laws

A Pick-like decomposition does not imply additivity, inclusion/exclusion, valuation structure, or a
scaling polynomial. Require the composition/Ehrhart assumptions independently.

## 8. Avoid false stochastic equivalence

Do not infer equal information from equal dimension, equal coordinates, one matching event
probability, or equal entropy. Use a justified decision-channel relation when the target depends on
information rather than one realized value.

## 9. Avoid invented approximation

Do not invent `ε`, priors, loss/regret functions, utilities, or risk tolerances to make approximate
sufficiency or numeric VOI available. Without authority, use exact/ordinal reasoning or `UNKNOWN`.

## 10. Avoid false procedure gaps

Normal owner failures, blockers, caveats, retries, or rejected states remain inside the owner when its
ontology already represents them soundly.

## 11. Avoid false no-owner claims

Search available validated ownership before deriving a new procedure. Manual invocation does not
supersede a known owner; explicit audit may test but cannot replace it.

## 12. Avoid audit capture

Do not run `PICK_AUDIT` before every mature owner, turn hypotheses into procedure authority, or let
audit coverage replace gap-local compile stopping. Audit remains explicit, bounded, and read-only.

## 13. Avoid contract accretion

A candidate distinction belongs in the contract only if it can change the target within the certified
domain and survives quotient/minimality analysis.

## 14. Avoid verifier-to-contract confusion

A broken measurement path does not imply a broken invariant. When a counterexample may be abstraction-
induced, classify it `REAL | SPURIOUS | UNKNOWN` before rebinding.

## 15. Avoid rule-patch rationalization

If a collapsed contrast proves the representation insufficient, do not special-case the decision rule
while knowingly keeping the same collapsed representation. Refine the representation with the
smallest witness-backed distinction.

## 16. Avoid expensive-information bias

More data is not automatically better. When several tests decide the same target, prefer the cheapest
reachable sufficient discriminator and stop.

## 17. Avoid presentation capture

Theorem names, role labels, certificates, and receipts are internal controls unless the user asks for
them or they clarify the answer. Do not turn improved internal reasoning into worse external prose.

## 18. Avoid compiler capture

The executor cannot reinterpret compiled semantics because a different local action appears
beneficial. Rebinding still requires the appropriate failure and structural explanation.

## 19. Degraded mode

If a necessary decision-relevant distinction cannot be observed reliably:

1. mark it unresolved;
2. block only decisions that depend on it;
3. authorize bounded evidence acquisition when allowed;
4. choose the cheapest target-separating observation when multiple options exist;
5. emit an escape condition if reliable reconstruction remains impossible.

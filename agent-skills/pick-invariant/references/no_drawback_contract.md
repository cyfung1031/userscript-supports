# No-Drawback Contract and Regression Evaluation

## 1. Status of the claim

"No drawback" is a bounded release claim, not a proof of universal model dominance. v11.0 established
a behavioral Pareto improvement over v10.1 on its declared matrix. **v11.1 is a hard compression
release over v11.0**: every protected behavioral dimension, scenario-quality row, synthetic execution
cost, maintainability score, and kernel activation must be non-regressing; mode/gate/reference
activation must remain exactly equivalent on the declared activation suite; context footprint must be
non-increasing on every declared path; and at least one context dimension must improve strictly.

A shorter kernel may not earn behavioral score credit merely for being shorter. Specialist doctrine
remains trigger-scoped, and savings may not come from suppressing a v11.0 activation.

## 2. Principle-preservation guards

### Authority parity
Known mature-owner tasks route directly unless exploration/audit was explicitly requested.
Exploration/audit never acquires mutation or procedure-installation authority.

### Target-preservation parity
A representation may discard raw detail only when the target decision and bound semantic
requirements remain reconstructible.

### Pick structural parity
Preserve `P/I/B/χ` as the dominant structural role grammar, applicability preconditions, residual
`χ`, observable lattice discipline, and literal-theorem boundary.

### Decision-quotient improvement
v11 must add explicit safe-merge/unsafe-merge, continuation-sensitive equivalence, and merge-based
minimality tests without weakening existing reconstruction/contrast rules.

### Probability parity and extension
Every v10.1 measure, transport, conditioning, quotient, canonicality, and distributional-sufficiency
safeguard remains reachable after a material stochastic trigger. v11 additionally distinguishes
stochastic information-channel sufficiency, authorized approximate sufficiency, and null-event
conditioning semantics.

### Composition improvement
Local-to-global reconstruction must not assume additivity merely because a Pick-like decomposition
exists. Valuation/Ehrhart/local-global gates are triggered only when their assumptions are material.

### Refinement improvement
Collapsed contrasts remain escalation witnesses. v11 additionally separates real counterexamples from
spurious abstraction-induced ones and refines the representation rather than special-casing a known
insufficient decision rule.

### Observation-economy improvement
When multiple observations could resolve the target, prefer the cheapest reachable discriminator that
separates acceptance-changing alternatives. Never fabricate priors/utilities to make this numeric.

### Presentation parity
The theorem stack is internal control logic by default. User-facing answers remain native-domain,
compact, and free of certificate/theorem ceremony unless the user requests or benefits from it.

## 3. Cost guards

Hard behavioral cost guards:

- no inherited mature-owner scenario may gain synthetic execution cost;
- no inherited fast path may be forced into full structural mode;
- new specialist capabilities must have explicit activation triggers;
- on the extended evaluation suite, a targeted gate should not be modeled as more expensive than the
  generic structural/probability fallback it replaces unless the extra cost is necessary and reported.

Hard v11.1 context-cost guards relative to v11.0:

- reduce always-loaded kernel wording without changing protected activations;
- keep theorem detail in the same lazy references;
- require every declared activated path to be no larger under transparent context proxies;
- require at least one strict kernel/context reduction;
- report words, characters, and bytes; do not claim exact model-token or latency savings without a
  matching tokenizer/benchmark.

## 4. Anti-ceremony tests

A conforming implementation must not:

- enumerate every `P/I/B/χ` field when irrelevant;
- print decision-equivalence classes or certificates merely to expose internal reasoning;
- run coverage for ordinary derivation;
- invoke specialist theorem gates because a keyword appears without target materiality;
- equate minimality with canonicality;
- equate symmetry with a canonical measure;
- force numeric VOI without supplied probabilities/losses;
- turn every verifier failure into a procedure failure;
- patch around a collapsed contrast while knowingly preserving the insufficient representation.

## 5. Claim monotonicity

Stronger claims require at least as much relevant authority/evidence/coverage as weaker claims:

```text
hypothesis < contrastable hypothesis < confirmed gap < adopted derivation authority
focused seam < changed-delta coverage < whole-artifact cleanliness
sufficient < removal/merge-tested minimal
one-event decision sufficiency < distributional sufficiency
symmetry < invariant measure < canonical-relative-to-G
```

No mode or claim transition moves rightward without the corresponding obligation.

## 6. Graceful degradation

If a required reference, oracle, runtime, fixture, loss metric, probability construction, or artifact
version is unavailable:

- preserve known owner results;
- preserve established evidence;
- identify the missing dependency;
- narrow the domain or downgrade the claim;
- use `UNKNOWN`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, or semantic underdetermination as appropriate.

Never silently substitute a weaker heuristic while claiming the stronger procedure ran.

## 7. Evaluation obligations

Every v11 release evaluation must include inherited v10.1 scenarios plus new families for:

1. safe/unsafe decision quotienting;
2. continuation-sensitive equivalence;
3. deletion and merge minimality;
4. stochastic information dominance/incomparability;
5. authorized versus unauthorized approximate sufficiency;
6. valuation/nonvaluation/local-global composition;
7. Ehrhart literal-extension boundary;
8. observation-value selection without fabricated numerics;
9. real versus spurious counterexamples;
10. representation refinement instead of rule patching;
11. null-event conditioning/disintegration;
12. invariant/canonical-relative symmetry claims;
13. theorem-stack presentation/anti-ceremony.

Measure at minimum:

```text
scenario quality
protected capability score
critical regression count
synthetic reasoning cost
inherited-path cost parity
maintainability/modularity
context proxies + activation equivalence (hard for v11.1)
```

## 8. Exact bounded Pareto gate

For v11.0 versus v10.1, retain the established behavioral gate. For v11.1 versus v11.0, the release
passes only if all are true on the declared deterministic matrix:

```text
for every protected capability c:
  score_v11.1(c) >= score_v11.0(c)

for every fixture f:
  quality_v11.1(f) >= quality_v11.0(f)
  synthetic_cost_v11.1(f) <= synthetic_cost_v11.0(f)

maintainability_v11.1 >= maintainability_v11.0
activation_vector_v11.1(f) == activation_vector_v11.0(f)
context_proxy_v11.1(f) <= context_proxy_v11.0(f)

and at least one context inequality is strict
```

For v11.1, strict improvement must come from efficiency, not an invented quality bonus. This is an
exact Pareto proof **relative to the declared matrix, activation oracle, and context proxies**. It is
not an empirical proof about every future model/task or an exact tokenizer/latency benchmark.

## 9. Sensitivity simulation

In addition to the exact matrix proof, perturb scenario prevalence, capability importance, and the
incremental effectiveness of new v11 rules. Report the distribution of v11-v10.1 aggregate score and
cost deltas.

The sensitivity simulation tests weight/assumption fragility. It does not convert a T1 design proxy
into a live-model benchmark.

## 10. Dominant-theorem comparison

The evaluation neighborhood must include at least one alternative-dominance candidate. A dominant
theorem is chosen for architecture/control coherence, not theorem prestige.

Pick dominance is retained only if the Pick-dominant candidate remains competitive under perturbation
and no alternative strictly dominates it on the protected matrix. A close score is acceptable when
Pick better preserves structural role decomposition and compatibility with the inherited skill.

## 11. Local Pareto stop

Declare a finite architectural neighborhood including relevant ablations and at least one ceremonial
always-on control. Evaluate all candidates with the same fixtures and scorer.

A valid stop reports:

- release versus parent exact Pareto status;
- which ablations fail which capabilities;
- whether an always-on theorem stack is dominated by trigger-scoped loading;
- dominant-theorem sensitivity;
- context footprint separately.

The claim is `ELEMENTWISE_BEHAVIORAL_PARETO_ON_DECLARED_MATRIX`, not global optimality.
